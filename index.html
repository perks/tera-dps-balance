Classic+ DPS Balance

Every DPS-role player in every recorded 5-man kill of Timescape (Hard/Savage), Shadow Sanguinary (Hard/Savage) and Dragon's Landing. Warriors are counted only when the meter flagged them as DPS, not tank. Healers, tanks, and entries under 50k DPS (died early / broken upload) are excluded.

Where each class stands

Each player's DPS is divided by the median DPS of that exact boss (all classes pooled), so a Savage kill and a Dragon's Landing kill are on one scale. 1.00 = boss median. Three lenses: raw, kill-time-matched, and same-gear.

Reading guide

  • Relative index is the fairest single number: it removes boss HP/difficulty differences. A class at 1.10 does 10% more than the typical DPS on the same boss.
  • Player-best median (in the raw lens table) counts each player once at their best run, so one player spamming 100 kills doesn't dominate a class.
  • Kill-time index is the same comparison inside duration buckets — it neutralises the "burst class only gets fast kills" and "sustained class only in slow parties" biases.
  • Same-gear index uses only players in the most common kit (+6 weapon, Chrono Brooch, +6 armor) — smaller sample, but gear is held constant.
  • Anonymous players are included in DPS stats but can't be de-duplicated; distinct-player counts are lower bounds.
